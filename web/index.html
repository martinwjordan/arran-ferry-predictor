Unofficial and still learning. Always check CalMac's service status before you travel.

Chance it runs is our estimate from the forecast wind and how often sailings were cancelled in similar forecasts since 2024, by ferry, plus a small allowance for technical and other cancellations. Today and the next two days show a percentage; further out shows Likely (75% or more), Uncertain or Unlikely (under 40%). CalMac's word always wins: a cancelled sailing shows as cancelled. Tap a sailing for the detail.

Wind is the strongest forecast mid-crossing during the sailing, in knots, with the direction it blows from. Gusts are coloured at 30 and 40 knots.
